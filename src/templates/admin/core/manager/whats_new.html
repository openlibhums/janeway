    Accessibility

    Accessibility pages for journals and presses

    Every journal and press site now has a dedicated accessibility page (repositories are not included). It includes a living Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT), which is maintained as part of Janeway and updated as the platform improves. You do not need to keep it up to date yourself.

    You can also add your own accessibility statement by writing one in Janeway or linking to an existing statement. You can do this for both journals and press sites.

     Action needed

    Add an accessibility statement, or link to an existing one, for your journal and for the press site if you manage it.

    Edit accessibility information

    Alt text for images

    You can now add alternative text to the images you upload in Janeway. Alt text is what a screen reader announces in place of an image, and it is essential for readers who cannot see the page.

    For this feature to help, you need to provide the text. Without alt text, assistive technology cannot describe an image to readers.

     Action needed

    Enter alt text when you upload images, and revisit the important images already on your site to add it.

    Go to Article Images Manager Go to Image Settings

    Introducing the Clarity theme

    This release includes a broad set of accessibility fixes across Janeway, and introduces Clarity, a new theme designed for accessibility from the ground up. The OLH, Material and Clean themes are being discontinued in favour of Clarity.

     Action needed

    If your journal still uses the OLH, Material or Clean theme, plan your move to Clarity.

    Change your journal theme

    Draft and published CMS pages

    Pages you create in the CMS now have a draft/published setting. Only published pages are listed in your site's sitemap, which is one of the ways Janeway meets the WCAG Multiple Ways success criterion by giving readers more than one way to find each page.

     Action needed

    Review your CMS pages and check each one is in the right state. Drafts are hidden from readers and left out of the sitemap.

    Go to Content Manager

    Getting in touch

    A new contact manager

    We have overhauled journal contact management. You can now:

    • link to specific contact people from other pages;
    • read contact messages in Janeway as well as by email;
    • edit the email template used to send contact messages.

     Optional

    Review your listed contacts and tailor the contact email template to your journal's voice.

    Go to Contact People

    Images

    Larger images without cropping

    Images uploaded to key fields can now be up to 1500×648 pixels and will display without being cropped, pixelated or distorted. Previously, these images were cropped to 750×324 pixels. This change applies to:

    • Article Large image file
    • Issue Large image
    • Journal Default large image
    • Press Default carousel image
    • News item Large image file
    • Repository Hero background

     Action needed

    Re-upload high-resolution (1500×648) versions of these images where you want a crisper display.

    Go to Image Settings

    Journal management

    Publishing status for journals

    A new Publishing status field lets you mark a journal as active, coming soon, archived or a test journal. This status controls whether the journal appears in journal lists, search indexes and archive feeds. The Hide from press field now works more consistently as well.

     Action needed

    Check that every journal on your press has the correct publishing status set.

    {% if request.user.is_staff %} Go to Journals in the admin {% else %}

    Publishing status is set by a press administrator in the Django admin.

    {% endif %}

    Workflow

    Typesetting replaces Production and Proofing

    Typesetting, which was previously available as a plugin, is now part of Janeway. It replaces the older Production and Proofing workflow stages. Those stages are deprecated: their settings no longer appear on the Manager page, and the stages themselves will be removed in a future release.
